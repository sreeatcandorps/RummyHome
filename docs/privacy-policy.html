Privacy Policy

Rummy Home · Last updated: August 2, 2026

Rummy Home (“the App”) is a scorekeeping app for friendly home rummy games. It does not process real-money wagers, payments, or gambling transactions. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address and a short passcode used to sign in.
  • Profile details: display name, optional phone number, and a unique player ID.
  • Game data: games you create or join, players in those games, round scores, expenses, and completion status.
  • Device/session data: authentication tokens needed to keep you signed in on your device.

How we use information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Let you find friends by email, phone, or player ID and invite them to games.
  • Store and sync game scores across devices for people in the same game.
  • Provide support and keep the service secure and reliable.

How information is stored and shared

Account and game data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with other players only as needed for game membership (for example, your name and player ID inside a shared game).

Permissions

The App may use SMS or share sheets when you choose to invite or share a player ID. Contact-book access is not required.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account and game records while your account is active so scores and history remain available. To request account or data deletion, contact us using the email below.

Children

Rummy Home is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact
