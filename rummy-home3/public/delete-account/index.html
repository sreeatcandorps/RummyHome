EZ Score: Delete your account

MindWeave Tech · EZ Score: Rummy

In the app (right away)

  1. Open EZ Score and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (the avatar tab).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read what is deleted and what stays, tick "I understand. This cannot be undone." and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted at once and the app signs you out.

By email

Write to support@mindweavetech.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my EZ Score account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. We may ask you to confirm from that address first, so nobody can delete your account for you.

Deleted

  • Your sign-in: email, passcode and every signed-in device.
  • Your phone number, Player ID, email settings and game defaults.
  • Your My players list, hidden-games list, queued emails and open invites.
  • Games you created that nobody else with an account was in.

Kept

  • Scorecards and results of games you played with other people keep your name and scores, like an unregistered player: no email, no phone, no Player ID.
  • Games you created that other players with accounts were in stay with them. If you were the only admin, the first other registered player becomes admin.

Why: a scorecard belongs to everyone who played it. Removing one column would change the other players' totals and results. Your name on it is the same as any player the organiser typed in by hand.

Questions

Anything else about your data: support@mindweavetech.com. The full privacy policy says what we collect and why.
