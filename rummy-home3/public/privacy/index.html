EZ Score: Privacy policy

MindWeave Tech · Effective November 12, 2026

EZ Score: Rummy ("EZ Score", "the app") is a scorekeeping app for home Rummy games, published by MindWeave Tech ("we"). This page says what the app stores about you, why, where it lives, who else touches it, and how to delete it. Questions: support@mindweavetech.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressYour sign-in. Also where game emails go, if you keep them on.
Name (and initials)Shown on the scorecards of games you are in, so the other players know whose column is whose.
Player ID (a number)What you give a friend so they can add you to a game. It is never used to sign in.
Phone number and postal code (optional)Only if you type them. Never texted, never shown to other players, not used for anything else today.
Games, rounds, scores and resultsThe point of the app. Visible to the people in the same game and to anyone you hand a share link to.
Your settingsGame defaults, email preferences, dark mode (dark mode stays on the phone).

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. There are no ads and no analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Emails we send

Two kinds, both optional and both on by default: when a game you are in starts (with a link to follow the scores live) and when a result you are in is saved (with a link to the result page). Each email has an unsubscribe link, and you can turn either kind off in the app under Profile > Email. Sign-in codes and password emails are sent only when you ask for them.

Share links

A live scorecard or a saved result can be shared with a link. Anyone with that link can see what the scorecard shows: first names or initials, scores and the date. No email, phone or Player ID is on those pages. Deleting the game or result makes the link show "deleted" instead.

Where the data lives and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in, in its ca-central-1 region (Canada). Access from the app is over HTTPS, and database rules limit every account to the games it is part of.
  • Resend delivers the emails above from scores@mindweavetech.com. It receives your email address and the content of each message.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone else. We would disclose data only if the law required it.

Security

All traffic between the app, the share site and the servers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passcodes are stored hashed. Contact details (email, phone, postal code) are readable only by your own account.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Your data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile > Account > Delete account, or by emailing support@mindweavetech.com from the address you signed up with. Full details, including exactly what is removed and what stays, are on the Delete account page.

In short: your sign-in, email, phone, Player ID, settings and lists are deleted right away. Scorecards and results of games you played with other people keep your name and scores, like an unregistered player, because those scores belong to everyone at the table.

Children

EZ Score is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
